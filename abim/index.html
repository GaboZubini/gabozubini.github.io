Architecture

The architectural model forms the foundation of our BIM workflow, defining the spatial organization, aesthetic vision, and functional requirements of the project.

Our architectural design integrates seamlessly with structural and MEP systems, ensuring coordination and clash detection throughout the design process.

Structure

The structural model encompasses all load-bearing elements including columns, beams, foundations, and lateral systems that ensure the building's stability and safety.

Advanced analysis and coordination with architectural elements guarantee optimal structural performance while maintaining design intent.

Precise modeling enables accurate quantity takeoffs and construction sequencing.

MEP Systems

Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing systems are fully coordinated in 3D, ensuring efficient routing and minimal conflicts with architectural and structural elements.

Our integrated approach optimizes system performance, energy efficiency, and maintainability throughout the building's lifecycle.

Detailed MEP modeling facilitates prefabrication and reduces on-site installation time.
